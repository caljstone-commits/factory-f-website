The Set Up

Mobile workshop,
on your doorstep.

A dealership-standard alloy refurb, delivered from a fully equipped mobile workshop.

Our purpose-built setup includes a contamination-free twin spray booth, dedicated wheel-prep area, on-board bead breaker, curing lamps, three-stage air filtration and a precision cutting lathe capable of handling wheels up to 24 inches, including deep dish rims*.

Every job is completed on-site, backed by full health and safety certification to main dealership standard — clean, precise and built to last.

*Deep dish rims may require additional assessment on a case-by-case basis.

We Evolve With The Times

Always investing,
always improving.

Vehicle technology moves fast — and we make sure our equipment keeps pace. We only partner with and invest in manufacturer-trusted tools, so every job is carried out with the same precision and care the manufacturers themselves demand.

Our latest partnership with Nuaxle brings innovative hub stands into our workshop, eliminating the risk of a car slipping off traditional jacking points or subframes. As manufacturers evolve their designs, locating a subframe accurately gets harder every year — especially with the large under-trays now common across modern EVs. One incorrectly placed jack or axle stand isn't just a scare; on an EV, it can mean battery damage costing thousands to repair.

By investing in the right tools for today's vehicles, not yesterday's, we protect your car exactly where manufacturers intend — no shortcuts, no guesswork.
