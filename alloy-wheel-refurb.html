Wheel Corrosion

Corrosion starts when the wheel's protective clear coat is chipped, scratched, or worn through — often from road salt, grit, and moisture getting underneath it. Once exposed, the bare alloy reacts with air and water, causing pitting, white filiform trails, and eventually flaking paint. Left untreated, it spreads and weakens the wheel's finish over time.
